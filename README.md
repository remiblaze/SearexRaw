# Searex Raw: one-knob tempo-synced chopper

![Searex Raw free one-knob tempo-synced chopper UI](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/SearexRaw/main/searexraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your loop chops harder.**

Searex Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Searex**, a tempo-synced rhythmic gate (chopper) for tech house and electronic music. Turn the CHOP knob and it locks to your host tempo, ducking a fixed 16-step pattern deeper and faster as you go, level-matched so it never jumps in volume.

**macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): AU, VST3, CLAP, AAX, Standalone. Signed and notarized by Apple.

**Windows** 10 and 11, 64-bit: VST3, CLAP, Standalone. Authenticode signed.

AAX ships on macOS only.

## 🚀 Download & Install

Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/SearexRaw/releases/latest) and pick your platform.

**macOS**
1. Download **`SearexRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
2. Double-click it and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized by Apple, so it installs cleanly with no security warnings.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Searex Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

**Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit**
1. Download **`SearexRaw_Installer.exe`**.
2. Run it and follow the installer. It is Authenticode signed.
3. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins. Searex Raw appears under **Remi Blaze**.

No dongle and no extra account on either platform.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **CHOP** knob drives an always-on, tempo-synced rhythmic gate. As you turn it up, the gate ducks harder and subdivides faster (from quarter-note down to sixteenth-note steps), following a fixed syncopated chop pattern with smooth, click-free step edges. Everything is level-matched, so pushing the knob changes the rhythm and intensity without a jump in loudness.

It locks to your host tempo automatically, and keeps chopping at 120 BPM when the transport is stopped or offline. That's the whole plugin: one knob, no menus to dig through.
