# Searex Raw — one-knob tempo-synced chopper

![Searex Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/SearexRaw/main/searexraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**One knob. Turn it up and your loop chops harder.**

Searex Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Searex** — a tempo-synced rhythmic gate (chopper) for tech house and electronic music. Turn the CHOP knob and it locks to your host tempo, ducking a fixed 16-step pattern deeper and faster as you go, level-matched so it never jumps in volume.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **CHOP** knob drives an always-on, tempo-synced rhythmic gate. As you turn it up, the gate ducks harder and subdivides faster (from quarter-note down to sixteenth-note steps), following a fixed syncopated chop pattern with smooth, click-free step edges. Everything is level-matched, so pushing the knob changes the rhythm and intensity without a jump in loudness.

It locks to your host tempo automatically, and keeps chopping at 120 BPM when the transport is stopped or offline. That's the whole plugin — one knob, no menus to dig through.
